# Reading List

This list contains starting references rather than a closed literature set. Add relevant papers as the project develops, and briefly state why each new source matters.

## Modular Robots and Docking

1. G. Liang, D. Wu, Y. Tu, and T. L. Lam, "Decoding modular reconfigurable robots: A survey on mechanisms and design," *The International Journal of Robotics Research*, 2025. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1177/02783649241283847) | [Open version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09743)
   - Starting review for connectors, actuators, homogeneity, and modular robot hardware.

## Origami Locking

2. S.-J. Kim, D.-Y. Lee, G.-P. Jung, and K.-J. Cho, "An origami-inspired, self-locking robotic arm that can be folded flat," *Science Robotics*, 2018. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/scirobotics.aar2915)
   - Core example for studying how origami geometry can create locking and deployed stiffness.

## Pneumatic Actuation and Deployable Origami

3. C. Liu, A. Orlofsky, C. D. Kitcher, and S. M. Felton, "A self-folding pneumatic piston for mechanically robust origami robots," *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, 2019. [Author PDF](https://samfelton.net/paperfiles/Liu_RAL19.pdf) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2019.2895881)
   - Experimental example combining pneumatic deployment, foldable structure, mechanical constraints, and actuator characterization.

4. S. Zare, A. Spaeth, S. Suresh, and M. Teodorescu, "Modular self-lock origami: Design, modeling, and simulation to improve the performance of a rotational joint," *Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers, Part K: Journal of Multi-body Dynamics*, 2024. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/14644193231216263)
   - Optional starting point for pneumatic pouch actuation, angular amplification, and mechanical-advantage trade-offs.
